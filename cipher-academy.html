How it works

Every cipher follows a carefully designed learning path. Morse starts with symmetrical patterns for intuition, then shifts to recognition-based progression inspired by the Koch method. Braille pairs each base shape with its extended counterpart, building from simple one-dot patterns to complex combinations. Pigpen traces the geometry of its grid. A spaced repetition engine schedules your reviews so symbols stick in long-term memory.

Two modes

New symbols introduced one at a time with immediate recall. A spaced repetition engine schedules your reviews so they stick.

Research-informed, transparently

Cipher Academy's learning paths draw on well-established ideas from cognitive science — spaced repetition, the Koch method, and recognition-based progression.

These principles are well-supported, but their specific implementation in Cipher Academy hasn't been validated through controlled studies — and we're upfront about that.
