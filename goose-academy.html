Built with respect

No ads. No subscriptions. No streaks or daily pressure. No tracking. The base game is free — one optional purchase unlocks everything, forever.

Goose Academy plays fully offline and runs entirely on your device. No accounts, no notifications; only the optional Google Play purchase ever uses the network.

How it works

  1. Answer short questions across the topics you choose.
  2. Goose notices which items you miss, confuse, or haven't seen in a while.
  3. Those items come back at the right moment — spaced out so they stick.
  4. Look-alike answers are used as distractors, so you learn the fine differences.
  5. Levels unlock gradually as you build mastery — entirely at your own pace.

A glimpse of practice

Research-informed, transparently

The practice loop draws on well-established ideas from cognitive science — spaced repetition, retrieval practice, interleaving, and learning from mistakes.

These principles are well-supported, but their specific implementation in Goose Academy has not yet been validated through controlled empirical studies — and we're upfront about that.
